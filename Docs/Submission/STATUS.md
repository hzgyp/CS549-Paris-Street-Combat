The latest team request makes six soldiers an initial configuration, not the final population limit, and adds intermediate mission objectives across a connected part of the existing city. The initial roster is one Allied player, two Allied NPCs and three German NPCs. Additional NPCs, finite groups and objective stages may be configured after pacing, navigation and performance evaluation. All NPCs share AI code with per-NPC team, role, group, patrol-route and search-zone data. The proposed pillars remain Animation, Collision Detection, Pathfinding and Navigation, and NPC AI / Behavior Trees; rendering and physical simulation support implementation.

The initial flow is initialize roster → Reach rally A → ClearArea B → Reach end C → success. Reach checks only the living player. ClearArea counts the living members of its assigned nonempty finite EnemyGroupID only after full registration; enemies leaving the area still count. Unique deaths count, including earlier kills; despawn and unloading do not. Only the current objective can advance, exactly once, and its condition is re-evaluated on activation. Player death during any playing stage causes failure. Full restart restores the configured roster, objective index, counters and timers. Defeated soldiers remain defeated across stages. Allies automatically follow/regroup and use distinct temporary support positions; Germans guard/patrol and search reachable points around their last seen target position for a bounded time. Objective points do not imply checkpoint/save reload.

Population expansion remains a proposed evaluation, not an implemented or approved final headcount. Compare small enemy increments against the initial six-soldier run and measure pacing, bottlenecks, AI responsiveness and frame times. Eight or ten total soldiers are exploratory test configurations. Before adding a group, require three successful full-mission runs covering both verified approaches where an alternate exists, no progression/navigation deadlocks, and performance within the declared budget. Set the simultaneous active-AI budget separately from the mission roster using measured costs, without hiding/despawning engaged actors or repopulating earlier casualties. Infinite waves and automatic difficulty increases are outside the plan.

The planned approach, intermediate objectives, end point and alternate route require an editor survey of actual connectivity, collision, sightlines, NavMesh coverage and travel time. No one-block boundary, fixed mission duration or fixed graph-node cap is assumed. Full-route, ally catch-up/bottleneck, sight-loss, no-teleport, actor-persistence, objective-order/group-counter and configurable-roster restart tests are planned, not passed. The existing documentation is a tools/material guide, not validated evidence of a playable route.
