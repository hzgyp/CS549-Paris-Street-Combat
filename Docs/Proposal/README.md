The current proposal selects **Animation, Collision Detection, Pathfinding and Navigation, and NPC AI / Behavior Trees**. Rendering and Physics and Dynamics support implementation. The initial roster is one Allied player, two Allied NPCs, and three German NPCs. Six is not a final population limit. All NPCs share AI code with individual Team, Role, EncounterGroupID, PatrolRouteID, and SearchZoneID configuration. Allies automatically follow/regroup and take distinct temporary support positions during contact. Germans guard/patrol and investigate reachable authored points around their last observed target position for a bounded time.

The initial flow is **initialize roster → Reach rally A → ClearArea B → Reach end C → success**. Reach checks only the living player; living allies follow and regroup. ClearArea waits for its nonempty finite group roster to be fully registered and the assigned EnemyGroupID to have zero living members. Unique deaths count, including kills before stage activation; leaving a volume, despawning or unloading does not count as death. The current stage re-evaluates its condition on activation and advances at most once. Player death during any playing stage causes failure. Success and failure offer a full mission restart that restores the configured roster, objective index, group counters, and timers. Objective points are progression markers; checkpoint/save reload is outside the MVP.

The new game flowchart appears in both reports and is available as [SVG](Visuals/paris-mission-flowchart.svg), [PNG](Visuals/paris-mission-flowchart.png), and [editable Mermaid source](Visuals/paris-mission-flowchart.mmd). It describes game rules rather than map geometry. The survey must record overhead captures, connectivity, collision, sightlines, NavMesh coverage and travel time before selecting actual locations, duration or graph size. An alternate route is included where editor inspection validates one. The surrounding city can remain visible without making every street or interior playable. No one-block boundary, fixed 60-90-second duration or 8-12-node graph cap governs this revision.

After the baseline works, keep two allies initially and trial small enemy increments, optionally followed by another finite group and objective stage. Eight or ten total soldiers are exploratory comparison configurations, not commitments. Before adding a group, require three successful full-mission runs covering both verified approaches where an alternate exists, no progression/navigation deadlocks, and performance within the declared budget. Use those results to select a total roster and simultaneous active-AI budget. Defeated actors stay defeated across stages; engaged actors are not hidden/despawned to meet a budget. Additional groups are finite and declared in mission data; infinite waves and automatic difficulty increases are outside this plan.
