The current proposal selects **Animation, Collision Detection, Pathfinding and Navigation, and NPC AI / Behavior Trees**. Rendering and Physics and Dynamics support implementation. The initial roster is one Allied player, two Allied NPCs, and three German NPCs. Six is not a final population limit. All NPCs share AI code with individual Team, Role, EncounterGroupID, PatrolRouteID, and SearchZoneID configuration. Allies automatically follow/regroup and take distinct temporary support positions during contact. Germans guard/patrol on foot and investigate reachable authored points around their last observed target position for a bounded time.

The generalized flow is **start/resume → current objective → progress update → next objective or completion**. Reach/Clear are example objective types; their order, map locations and checkpoint sites are not fixed. We propose saves at selected safe objective boundaries. Retry restores the latest objective/player/NPC snapshot, falling back to the start if none exists; full new-mission restart remains available. Saving alone does not heal, refill ammunition or revive casualties. Resupply and reinforcement rules remain open. During ordinary forward progression, casualties and ammunition persist.

Navigation uses UE NavMesh/MoveTo as the baseline. The team authors destination assignment, local avoidance, bottleneck waiting and bounded replanning. A custom tactical A* layer is optional if instructor feedback or gameplay needs justify it; the assignments do not mandate a custom solver. A squad coordinator assigns distinct goals while each NPC keeps separate controller/Blackboard state and perception. Patrol means walking among waypoints. Version-control tooling is undecided; the proposals do not prescribe Git/LFS.

The new game flowchart appears in both reports and is available as [SVG](Visuals/paris-mission-flowchart.svg), [PNG](Visuals/paris-mission-flowchart.png), and [editable Mermaid source](Visuals/paris-mission-flowchart.mmd). It describes game rules rather than map geometry. The survey must record overhead captures, connectivity, collision, sightlines, NavMesh coverage and travel time before selecting actual locations, duration or navigation configuration. An alternate route is included where editor inspection validates one. The surrounding city can remain visible without making every street or interior playable. No one-block boundary, fixed 60-90-second duration or 8-12-node graph cap governs this revision.

After the baseline works, trial small finite NPC additions and record pacing, bottlenecks, responsiveness and frame times. Use those results to select a total roster and simultaneous active-AI budget. Additional groups should have declared mission roles; do not hide engaged actors or silently replace casualties to satisfy a budget. Infinite waves and automatic difficulty increases remain outside the plan. The main challenge is integrating UI and physical character/environment interactions into the city while NPC decisions and movement remain coordinated as numbers grow.
