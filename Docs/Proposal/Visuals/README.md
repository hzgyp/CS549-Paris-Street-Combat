## Current proposal visuals

Both proposals embed [`paris-six-character-concept.png`](paris-six-character-concept.png), regenerated on 23 September 2026 with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool. Both unchanged official Meshingun Studio gallery images, `paris-environment-01.jpg` and `paris-environment-02.jpg`, were supplied as image references for architecture, materials and visual density. They remain local source references and are not separately embedded in the reports. The [exact generation record](STREET_MOCKUP_PROMPT.md) identifies the inputs and prompt.

The new 2172 x 724 panoramic concept depicts a first-person city junction with two Allied NPCs and three German NPCs at different distances. Including the player, this represents the initial six soldiers, not a final population limit. It suggests movement through connected streets without claiming an exact vendor floorplan. It is newly generated concept art, not an edited supplier screenshot, Unreal capture, historical photograph or proof of implemented gameplay.

Both proposals embed `paris-mission-flowchart.png`, the formal game flowchart generated by [Tools/build_mission_flowchart.py](../../../Tools/build_mission_flowchart.py). The builder produces [SVG](paris-mission-flowchart.svg), [PNG](paris-mission-flowchart.png), and [Mermaid source](paris-mission-flowchart.mmd). This is an AI-assisted conceptual visual rendered deterministically in code from the user's requested design direction; it is not an ImageGen output or a reconstruction from vendor images.

The diagram was revised on 23 September 2026 to show general objective progression rather than a fixed three-stage mission: start/resume, pursue a Reach/Clear objective until its condition passes, update progress, then continue or complete. The proposed checkpoint scheme saves at selected safe boundaries; player death retries the latest snapshot or starts fresh when none exists. Saving does not automatically heal, refill ammunition or revive casualties. Objective order, checkpoint sites and resupply/reinforcement rules remain open. This is game logic, not vendor geography. Exact streets and connections require an Unreal editor survey. Earlier ImageGen images remain provenance only and are no longer embedded. See [the visual creation record](MOCKUP_PROMPTS.md).

## Superseded text only street concept

`paris-six-character-concept-text-only-v1.png` contains the original two concept views generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool on 23 September 2026. The upper view shows the first-person player with two Allied NPCs and three German NPCs; the lower schematic uses three blue and three red position markers. The image was generated from a textual description, without vendor artwork as an image input. It is illustrative concept art, not an Unreal capture, historical evidence, or a guarantee of the purchased assets' appearance.
