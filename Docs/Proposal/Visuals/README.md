## Current proposal visuals

### Jingdi adjustment sketches — 20 October 2026

The [mapped mission envelope and minimap sketch](adjustment-jingdi-20261020-playable-map.svg) and [six-stage campaign sketch](adjustment-jingdi-20261020-campaign-route.svg) accompany [the adjustment suggestions](../Adjustment_Jingdi_20261020.md). Rebuild them with `python Tools/build_adjustment_sketches_20261020.py` from the project root.

These are editable, self-contained SVG diagrams with vector annotations over an unchanged embedded copy of the team-authored [25 cm expanded L0 survey](../../Images/MissionLoopV1/PARIS_GRID_25CM_EXPANDED_L0_20261007.png), cropped through its SVG viewBox to X = 5–145 m and Y = −225…−18 m. Jingdi's supplied images 1 and 3 guide the minimap-coverage suggestion and route arrangement; neither screenshot is altered or copied into a new gameplay capture. The minimap inset is illustrative and uses the same survey coordinates as its marked map window.

The revised [layout data](adjustment-jingdi-20261020-layout.json) defines six coloured encounter areas, shared route endpoints and 66 proposed enemy positions: 10, 10, 12, 10, 12 and 12. C1 now proposes an expanded campaign encounter. Colour fills are clipped to the same cardinally connected white-cell survey component; each enemy triangle is placed on a white candidate centre inside its area. The route uses the far-eastern bypass instead of the disconnected old C2–C3 shortcut. Red crosses identify excluded shortcut locations. It finishes on the northeastern street near (110, −35 m), replacing the earlier unsupported endpoint and westward detour.

The builder reads the unchanged survey, checks every cardinal route segment and shared stage endpoint against white-cell connectivity, and checks marker counts and candidate positions. This is only a 2D raster check: the data includes narrow sampled connections and does not establish native support/link connectivity, NavMesh or actual squad passage. Dashed borders are proposed encounter extents; enclosed black areas are not admitted terrain or a deletion inventory. White survey centres are candidates; black includes blocked, unknown or unadmitted space. The sketches add no runtime or human acceptance. SVG metadata records the unchanged background's SHA-256; no engine, original asset or registry is modified. The earlier sketches and proposal were retained under `tmp/jingdi-route-revision-20261020/before/` before this correction.

Both proposals embed [`paris-six-character-concept.png`](paris-six-character-concept.png), regenerated on 23 September 2026 with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool. Both unchanged official Meshingun Studio gallery images, `paris-environment-01.jpg` and `paris-environment-02.jpg`, were supplied as image references for architecture, materials and visual density. They remain local source references and are not separately embedded in the reports. The [exact generation record](STREET_MOCKUP_PROMPT.md) identifies the inputs and prompt.

The new 2172 x 724 panoramic concept depicts a first-person city junction with two Allied NPCs and three German NPCs at different distances. Including the player, this represents the initial six soldiers, not a final population limit. It suggests movement through connected streets without claiming an exact vendor floorplan. It is newly generated concept art, not an edited supplier screenshot, Unreal capture, historical photograph or proof of implemented gameplay.

Both proposals embed `paris-mission-flowchart.png`, the formal game flowchart generated by [Tools/build_mission_flowchart.py](../../../Tools/build_mission_flowchart.py). The builder produces [SVG](paris-mission-flowchart.svg), [PNG](paris-mission-flowchart.png), and [Mermaid source](paris-mission-flowchart.mmd). This is an AI-assisted conceptual visual rendered deterministically in code from the user's requested design direction; it is not an ImageGen output or a reconstruction from vendor images.

The diagram was revised on 23 September 2026 to show general objective progression rather than a fixed three-stage mission: start/resume, pursue a Reach/Clear objective until its condition passes, update progress, then continue or complete. The proposed checkpoint scheme saves at selected safe boundaries; player death retries the latest snapshot or starts fresh when none exists. Saving does not automatically heal, refill ammunition or revive casualties. Objective order, checkpoint sites and resupply/reinforcement rules remain open. This is game logic, not vendor geography. Exact streets and connections require an Unreal editor survey. Earlier ImageGen images remain provenance only and are no longer embedded. See [the visual creation record](MOCKUP_PROMPTS.md).
