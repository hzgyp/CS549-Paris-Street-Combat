## Delivered behaviour

One player, two Allied NPCs and three Germans operate in a persistent32x22m exercise. Canvas clicks issue a real player path; dragging a shape pauses and validates a new position. The server recomputes sensing, private observations, roles, bounded paths and actual swept motion at fixed0.1s ticks. Three presets expose following, distinct Support/Overwatch reservations and discovery/lost-sight search. Allied formation goals settle after the player stops. Accepted new player orders reopen the finite planning budget and preserve German memory/search deadlines.

German sight includes range, heading/FOV and wall occlusion. Losing sight revokes engagement permission immediately on placement sensing, or at the next0.2s sensor tick during movement. A0.6s loss grace precedes Investigate/Search at a frozen last-observed location. Search has a fixed6s deadline and at most four points, then Return/Guard; unseen current player positions do not redirect it. The displayed FOV polygon is clipped at exercise walls. Engagement is intent only: no damage/ammunition transaction. Hearing events, death/checkpoints, suppression effectiveness and UE behaviour were not exercised.

The existing16 Paris black/white and status rasters are authenticated reference backdrops with exact audit byte/hash checks. They do not create the exercise walls or its connectivity. Black includes unknowns; surface ordinals are not floors; saved/expanded scopes remain separate. This is a working geometry simulation, not a measured Paris navigation/LOS simulation.

Run/pause,0.5s steps, speed, member inspection, paths, observations/deadline, role scores and actual arrival are available. Timeline review freezes editing. Layout plus desired-effect note is local-storage persistent; loading it starts a new episode. Refresh/navigation recover an existing paused simulation within the same authenticated browser session. Navigating away stops stepping. Service restart invalidates in-memory worlds/browser cookies; saved layouts and the retained platform bootstrap link survive. No automatic background progression.
