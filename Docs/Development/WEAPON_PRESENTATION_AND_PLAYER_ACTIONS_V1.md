## Shot feedback design

Flash, impacts and marks are VFX/decal feedback, separate from body animation. Emit one authoritative shot payload carrying shot/generation identity, muzzle transform and the final blocking hit actor/component, point, normal and surface classification. Rejected input produces no effects. An accepted discharge produces one flash even on a miss; an obstruction uses the actual blocking contact, never the intended camera target behind a wall. Effects never decide damage or ammo. Preserve once-only ammo/damage and guarded reload transactions.

Prefer verified existing content. If absent, mark the dedicated gap and implement bounded simple flash/dust/spark as separate team VFX work; do not call it an existing purchased effect. Bullet trails are optional cosmetics, not damage or impact authority.

Classify masonry/plaster/concrete, metal, wood, glass and unknown default. Inspect actual trace physical-material and section support first: visual material names do not prove hit queries return useful surface data. Use narrow team PhysicalMaterial derivatives or explicit component/section mappings where supported, not a broad vendor-material resave. Unknown or unsupported multi-material face identification must use the neutral fallback and be logged in testing.

Masonry uses dust/chips and a small matching mark; metal uses spark/mark candidates; wood needs splinter/wood-mark selection; glass gets distinct impact feedback, not a new destruction system. Matching effects not found remain marked gaps; one concrete mark cannot pass all-surface acceptance. Do not project wall decals onto animated characters. A blood system is not implied.

Proposed initial bounds: 32 active transient effects, 128 marks, effect lifetime up to 2 s and mark lifetime 30 s. Pool/expire old feedback, clear it on reset and invalidate stale generations. These are proposed limits, not measured performance. Test normals, scale, corners, moving components, unsupported decal surfaces and repeated fire.
