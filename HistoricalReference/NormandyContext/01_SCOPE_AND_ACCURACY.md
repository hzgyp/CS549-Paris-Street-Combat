# Scenario Baseline and Accuracy Rules

## Location, forces and time

Use **Omaha, 6 June 1944** as the working research boundary. Keep the game a compact, explicitly compressed encounter rather than claiming to reconstruct the entire landing. The official assault plan places the 116th Infantry on the western side and the 16th Infantry on the eastern side. Actual landings were scattered; a planned landing coordinate does not prove where a squad arrived. [S01](SOURCES.md)

The player unit, exact German strongpoint and mission start time remain open. Use a U.S. infantry / German Heer placeholder pairing until those decisions are locked. Do not assign a named German company or weapon emplacement merely from a generic division map. Early Allied assessments in a campaign account may differ from the actual German disposition.

## Geography must drive the art

The local photographs document a tidal flat, shingle, beach exits and varying bluff slopes. Build the chosen terrain cross-section before placing decorative bunkers. A bunker can fire along the beach; its opening need not point directly toward the sea. Keep scene visibility, collision and cover effectiveness consistent with the geometry. [S01](SOURCES.md)

| Common mistake | Project rule |
|---|---|
| Turning all of Omaha into vertical Pointe du Hoc cliffs | Use the selected Omaha sector map and slope references |
| Copying a post-invasion road, wreck or harbor into the first assault | Record the photograph date and construction chronology |
| Filling the shoreline with interchangeable fortresses | Give the one mission bunker a referenced orientation and silhouette |
| Placing dense inland hedgerows on the open tidal flat | Separate the beach, bluff and inland landscape zones |
| Mixing Utah airborne uniforms with ordinary Omaha assault infantry | Select one unit and role before choosing clothing |
| Using British, Canadian and U.S. flags/patches interchangeably | Keep faction, service, unit and rank as separate asset fields |
| Reusing AI concept details as historical proof | Treat concept art as composition research only |

## First art approval set

Prepare one U.S. rifleman, one U.S. squad leader, one Heer rifleman and one Heer NCO as turnarounds. Each needs front/back/side clothing, helmet, footwear, web equipment, visible rank positions, weapon and a dated source link. Only then produce variations. Select ordinary front-line ranks for the encounter; senior officers belong in context material unless the scenario documents their presence.

The existing four-allies/two-defenders final-mission target concerns core interactive characters. The 21 September team decision allows additional background landing troops; G1 currently retains three core companions. A rank distinction changes identity and behavior responsibility, not a health or damage multiplier. Authenticity should come from coherent details, not extra content volume.
