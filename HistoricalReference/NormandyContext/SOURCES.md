## S39 — U.S. Type II service shoes, Paris Musées

Musée de la Libération de Paris / Musée du général Leclerc / Musée Jean Moulin, object 2002.1903C, attributed anonymous and dated 1941–45 · period/general artifact in a modern collection · accessed 2026-09-22

[Museum record](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/en/node/942801), [IIIF manifest](https://apicollections.parismusees.paris.fr/iiif/600003391/manifest). The record describes an American service-shoe pair with applied toe, rubber heel and forepart, and laces. Two CC0 museum images are archived at [detail](images/uniforms/service_shoes_type_ii/view_1.jpg) and [soles](images/uniforms/service_shoes_type_ii/view_2.jpg). They are construction references, not scene-specific proof for Omaha on 6 June 1944. The model geometry is newly authored; the museum photographs are not used as a game texture.
