## Smallest useful supplement

1. **Craft and ramp reference sheet:** period interior/front-ramp photos, component boundaries, and a human-scale graybox. Use real references to resolve construction; an AI detail view cannot establish hinges, dimensions or material authenticity.
2. **Shoreline and first-cover sheet:** a simple terrain cross-section plus dry sand, wet sand and shingle samples, including the transition and the cover silhouette at player eye level. Reuse the existing G0 material lab for the first engine comparison.
3. **Reusable obstacle sheet:** front/side/three-quarter reference views for only the first obstacle types being built; keep source and scene-placement assumptions visible.

Keep the existing scene compositions. Correct the craft material and shingle scale when revising the concepts or building the first detailed assets. First-person weapon/hand close-ups also need a verified asset/animation sample, but that need not block terrain and craft blockout.

The linked historical images are reference evidence, not automatically approved distributable game textures. The Poly Haven entries are downloadable asset candidates whose pages state CC0; Coast Sand 01 color, DirectX normal and roughness maps have now been downloaded at 1K and imported into the first asset kit; the other candidates remain unacquired. No historical match or runtime performance result is established. No purchase or marketplace compatibility claim is made.
