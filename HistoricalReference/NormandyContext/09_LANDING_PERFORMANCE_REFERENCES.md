## Historical constraints that change the script

S01 describes heavy equipment, seasickness, water depth and poor footing limiting movement; it explicitly records walking as well as men seeking low shelter. It also describes fire reaching craft before unloading and the disappointing effect of the prelanding air bombardment. Those observations rule out a universal dry-land sprint in deep water and the assumption that beach defenses had already been cleared by aircraft. See the archived `omaha3.html`, printed pp. 41–49. They do not require this project to depict every boat or soldier identically.

The design response is to replace the relaxed unarmed walk with context: low boat waiting; difficult wading; urgent short advances on suitable ground; crouching/prone shelter; and crawling only where the body can remain above water and the terrain permits it. This is an authored, compressed encounter. Its timing and exact poses below are project choices.
