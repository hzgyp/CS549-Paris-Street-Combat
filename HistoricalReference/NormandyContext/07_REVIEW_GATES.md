## Review process

1. Give every hero asset a source ID, date, place/unit scope and intended use.
2. Compare silhouette, clothing/gear combination, insignia placement and weapon/vehicle variant.
3. Record a screenshot of the actual game asset beside its references. A source link alone is insufficient.
4. Mark each claim `Verified for scenario`, `Period-compatible / placement unverified`, `Design approximation`, or `Blocked pending evidence`.
5. Only the first category may be described as a scenario-specific reconstruction. The others remain visible in the development record.
