# Vehicles, Landing Craft and Aircraft

## Tanks: variant first, visual detail second

The [6 June afternoon Omaha photograph](images/vehicles/sherman_omaha_19440606.jpg) establishes a Sherman in the beach environment. It does not, by itself, settle the unit, hull subvariant or the configuration of the player encounter's tank. The official landing account distinguishes the 741st and 743rd Tank Battalions and different landing outcomes; do not present all amphibious tanks as having the same fate. [S01 and S10](SOURCES.md)

For an approved Sherman asset, record hull type, gun/turret, suspension, amphibious equipment if any, stowage, unit markings and condition. A DD flotation screen is equipment, not a generic tarp. An ordinary tank model cannot be converted into a documented DD variant solely by adding fabric.

**Museum warning:** The Tank Museum's DD exhibit is a later M4A2 example that did not take part in D-Day; the museum identifies other Sherman variants used for D-Day DDs. Its Firefly and later 76-mm examples likewise require their own date/unit checks. A museum restoration is valuable for form, but not automatic proof of a June 6 U.S. Omaha configuration. [Tank Museum](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/m4-sherman/)

| Candidate | Initial project treatment |
|---|---|
| U.S. Sherman with documented 1944 configuration | One static or damaged background asset after variant review |
| Sherman DD | Optional only with a selected battalion and landing context |
| British Firefly | Not part of the default U.S. Omaha encounter |
| German armored vehicle | No beach tank counterattack is approved by the current evidence |
| Late or postwar vehicle variant | Exclude unless a new dated scenario explicitly supports it |

Do not add Tiger II, Pershing, a later Sherman configuration, or a generic tank battle merely to increase visual spectacle. This is a scope exclusion as well as an accuracy gate. Vehicle combat and drivable tanks remain outside the baseline.

The [German Panther/Tiger recognition plate](images/vehicles/german_panther_tiger_reference.png) is included for comparison. It comes from the 1945 U.S. handbook, with unspecified photograph dates, and is not proof of either vehicle at the selected beach/time. Keep it in the reference library rather than treating it as permission to add enemy tanks.

## Landing craft and battlefield vehicles

Use the [LCVP landing photograph](images/beach/omaha_landing_visibility.jpg) to check ramp, gunwale, troop scale and shoreline relationship. LCVP, LCT and LST are different craft, not interchangeable sizes of one boat. Preserve source captions before modeling a specific hull number. Vehicle presence in an afternoon or later photograph must not be silently shifted to the first assault wave.

## Aircraft: retain role and time

The local [B-26 image](images/aircraft/b26_invasion_stripes.jpg) supplies a period aircraft silhouette and invasion-stripe reference. Its exact date and flight location are unresolved; the filename is not evidence of a particular sortie. [Source record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:B-26_pre-D-Day.jpg)

A [Fw 190A photograph](images/aircraft/fw190a_captured_shape_only.jpg) supplies the German aircraft's basic shape, but depicts a captured aircraft tested in Maryland in spring 1944. It wears **U.S. Navy camouflage and U.S. markings**, with armament apparently removed. Those markings must never be copied as a Luftwaffe combat scheme. Its source date field and descriptive month differ; both are retained in the catalog. [S26](SOURCES.md)

| Aircraft family / role | What to verify before inclusion |
|---|---|
| B-26 medium bomber | Model, squadron, stripe layout, operational time and target; no continuous low-level beach orbit by assumption |
| Allied fighter / naval spotting aircraft | Specific type and mark, service, unit and sortie context; NHHC documents spotting operations over the American beaches |
| C-47 transport | Transport/airborne context and route; do not imply Omaha assault infantry landed by parachute |
| German fighter | A dated, location-specific event is required before scripting a recognizable attack |

Use [USAF museum context](https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/1789416/operation-overlord-d-day/) and [NHHC operational history](https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-031/h-031-1.html) to narrow the next search. The one B-26 picture is not a complete multi-aircraft modeling pack. Precise model sheets for additional aircraft are deliberately still open.

For the first build, distant aircraft can be omitted. If later included, use a bounded, reviewed background pass with no flight combat or player anti-aircraft system. Absence is preferable to an unsupported close-up centerpiece.
