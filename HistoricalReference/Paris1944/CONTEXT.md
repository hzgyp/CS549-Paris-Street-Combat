# Paris, August 1944 - historical production context

## Narrative baseline

The playable encounter is fictionalized within the liberation period in August 1944. Normandy on 6 June supplies campaign background only. Avoid suggesting the player steps off a Normandy beach directly into Paris on the same day.

The Musee de la Liberation describes the uprising, resistance organization and barricades from 19 August through the 26 August parade; it records nearly 600 barricades in Paris and its nearby urban area on 24 August. These civic and resistance elements matter to the historical setting even when complex civilian simulation is out of production scope. [P01](https://www.museeliberation-leclerc-moulin.paris.fr/en/museum/la-liberation-de-paris)

The American 4th Infantry Division participated in the liberation of Paris and had landed at Utah Beach on D-Day. Selecting that formation would require correcting any inherited Omaha unit identity. The French 2nd Armored Division and FFI are separate identity/reference tracks; similar American-looking equipment does not establish unit affiliation. [P03](https://achh.army.mil/history/book-wwii-huertgenforest-4thidsurgannual1944/) and [P04](https://www.army.mil/article/165476/1st_sbct_honors_crossing_of_the_rhine_river)

No player unit or enemy formation is selected by this document. Choose identities alongside compatible licensed assets, then verify uniforms, insignia and weapons. Do not invent a documented combat event for a fictionalized asset-pack street.

## Street and environment use

The Paris municipal event map provides locations and episodes for reference, including command posts, barricades and the Grand Palais. Use it to distinguish actual sites from the asset pack's generic city composition. [P02](https://www.paris.fr/en/pages/relive-the-liberation-of-paris-through-an-interactive-map-36122)

The Paris Musees collection entry for a barricade at rue de la Huchette / place du Petit-Pont is dated 23 August 1944. It is a useful date-and-place-specific visual reference, not evidence for the layout of every Paris street. [P05](https://parismuseescollections.paris.fr/en/node/132774)

Buildings, posters, street signs, civilian vehicles, tank variants, rubble density, flags and military insignia need a selected-scene review. Do not use aircraft/paratrooper VFX from the environment package simply because it contains them. Limit alterations to historical mismatches visible in the bounded encounter rather than reconstructing Paris.

## Weather and time

Use fixed lighting. No precise date-specific temperature, humidity, wind or rain values have been established in this source set. Keep them unknown. Normandy D-Day weather and the film Pressure are not evidence for August Paris conditions.

## Evidence standard

Record title, institution, event date/location, URL, image rights and limitations. Distinguish fighting photographs from celebrations, later photographs, reenactments and asset marketing. Do not infer a unit, weapon variant, exact street geometry or meteorological reading from an ambiguous image. Preserve the distinction between historical inspiration and verified reconstruction in the final presentation.
